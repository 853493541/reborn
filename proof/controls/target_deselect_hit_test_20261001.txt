Problem: the click pick used a 12-degree cone around the entity direction from the camera,
so clicks NEAR the dummy (not on it) still selected/kept the target - "some places are not
clicking off". The user's rule: any click not on the target is a deselect.

Fix (client/Targeting.cs Pick): ray vs each entity's vertical body cylinder (radius 90 u,
height 220 u from the entity base), nearest positive ray hit wins; any ray that misses every
body is an empty pick -> deselect. The real client picks the character model
(KCharacter::OnPickPrepare / represent PickDoodad); the host has no world->screen/model pick,
so the body cylinder is the host's hit volume (MED approximation, documented in
docs/controls/JX3_TARGET_SELECTION.md section 6).
