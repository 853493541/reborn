## Design notes / warts
#  - The panel is a SEPARATE top-level window (WS_EX_NOACTIVATE, owned by the
#    main form), NOT a child of the render host: adding WinForms controls to the
#    render panel before engine init hung the host; even the top-level window is
#    created LAZILY in the frame loop (after Init3DEngine), never at startup.
#  - Form.BackColor must be opaque: Color.FromArgb(alpha,...) throws
#    "Control does not support transparent background colors" (fixed).
